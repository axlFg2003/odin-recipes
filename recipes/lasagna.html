Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a type of pasta, possibly one of the oldest types,[2] made in very wide, flat sheets. In Italian cuisine it is made of stacked layers of pasta alternating with fillings such as ragù (ground meats and tomato sauce), béchamel sauce, vegetables, cheeses (which may include ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan), and seasonings and spices.

Ingredients

  • lasagna noodles
  • ground meat
  • tomato sauce
  • ricotta cheese
  • mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Garlic
  • Onions
  • Herbs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
